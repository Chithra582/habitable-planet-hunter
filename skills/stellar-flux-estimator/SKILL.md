# Stellar Flux Estimator Skill

## Overview
Derives relative stellar flux, orbital boundaries, and radiation equilibrium states directly from fundamental astronomical observables without relying on pre-computed flux indices.

## Operations
1. Calculates incident stellar flux scaling from host star luminosity and orbital semi-major axis.
2. Evaluates periastron and apastron flux extremes for eccentric orbits.
3. Computes tidal locking limit distances based on stellar mass and planetary age.
4. Categorizes stellar spectral classification and effective thermal ranges.
